# WarGames expansion references

The Joshua terminal expansion draws on the interaction structure and terminal
sequences of **WOPR**, copyright (c) 2026 Christopher Goes,
https://github.com/GhostofGoes/WOPR, provided locally as WOPR-main.
Its MIT license and upstream notices are retained in `third_party/wopr/`.

The local **WarGames WOPR Computer Simulation**, copyright (c) 2022 David Lawson,
was also inspected as a design reference. Its source has not been embedded.

WOPR's MIT grant covers its code, not the film's screen text. Recognizable film
terminal quotations remain the property of their respective rights holders.
This is an unofficial fan simulation, with no affiliation or endorsement by the
film's owners. The map and scenario names here are newly written. This adaptation
does not include WOPR's third-party map or the abs0 connection-header and scenario
transcriptions described in the upstream notice.
