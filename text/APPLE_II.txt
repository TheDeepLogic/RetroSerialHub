APPLE II
========

The Apple II was introduced by Apple Computer in 1977. Designed by Steve
Wozniak, it helped move personal computing from electronics hobbyists into
homes, schools, and small businesses.

The original model used a MOS Technology 6502 processor and shipped with 4
kilobytes of memory in its base configuration. Its color graphics and
composite video output made it possible to connect the computer to a
television or monitor. Programs and data could initially be loaded from
audio cassette. A built-in version of Integer BASIC gave users a way to
write and run programs as soon as the computer started.

Expansion slots were an important part of the design. Owners could add
memory, serial interfaces, and other cards, extending the computer for new
uses. Apple's Disk II floppy drive made storing and loading programs more
convenient than cassette. Later Apple II models added features and memory
while keeping much of the original system's compatibility.

The Apple II became a significant platform for educational software,
productivity programs, and games. Its long-lived design and broad selection
of expansion hardware helped establish the personal computer as a practical
tool for more than electronics enthusiasts.
